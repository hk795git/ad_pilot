City Leaders Debate New Transit Plan Amid Rising Costs

By Staff Reporter · Pilot Article

Officials outlined competing proposals as commuters voiced concerns about reliability and affordability.

The city’s transportation committee met Tuesday to review options that would expand service while addressing maintenance backlogs.

Several members argued the plan should prioritize stations with the highest congestion. Others said equity goals should guide the investment strategy.

Analysts noted that budget constraints could delay construction unless officials secure additional funding from state and federal sources.

What happens next

The committee will vote next month. If approved, the plan moves to the city council for a final decision.

Public hearings are expected to continue through the end of the quarter.

Advocates said clearer timelines and performance targets would help rebuild public trust.
